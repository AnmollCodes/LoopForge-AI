Zone 02

Prompting was the old job. Watching is the new one.

For two years, using AI meant being the loop yourself — typing, waiting, checking, repeating. Loop engineering moves that repetition into the system: a schedule takes the trigger, memory takes the repetition, and you take only the decision that actually needs a human — approve, or don't.

Zone 03

What actually happens on every run

This is a real sequence, not a mood board — the order matters, because each step depends on the one before it.

01 — scan

Reads the boards

Pulls fresh postings from RemoteOK, Arbeitnow, and any Greenhouse boards you name — all public, free, no key.

02 — remember

Checks its memory

Every job ID it's ever seen lives in a small local file. Nothing gets shown to you twice.

03 — fit

Scores two ways

Keyword matching plus a local TF-IDF semantic-similarity signal — catches paraphrases keywords alone would miss.

04 — draft + review

Writes, then checks itself

Drafts a tailored note, then a second pass reviews it against a rubric and repairs anything that fails before you ever see it.

05 — gate

Waits for you

Every match lands as "pending review." Nothing goes out until you press approve.

The one rule that never bends: this loop watches, filters, and drafts. It does not apply, send, or spend on your behalf. The final approval is always yours.

A sixth agent runs on demand, not on schedule: paste a resume, and the Profile Builder Agent extracts your real must-have and nice-to-have keywords automatically — transparent, with exact counts, nothing saved until you confirm.
